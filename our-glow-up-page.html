Piercing FAQ

DO YOU USE A NEEDLE OR A PIERCING DEVICE

Claire's uses a hand-pressured, FDA-registered medical device operated by a trained Piercing Specialist. Benefits of device piercing include: A safe, controlled, hygienic experience Approachable for guests uncomfortable with needles - especially children A quick, standardized experience across locations Jewelry designed to support easier changes (e.g. needle piercing flatbacks can be harder to change out on your own)

IS EAR PIERCING AT CLAIRE'S SAFE?

Yes. When performed by a trained Piercing Specialist following proper hygiene and safety procedures, ear piercing is safe. Claire's follows established sanitation protocols, uses sterile, single-use piercing components, and the piercing device itself is an FDA-registered Class 1 medical device. You'll also get detailed aftercare instructions - following that routine is a key part of a safe, successful aftercare experience.
